HR Elevation = Pain Signal
Heart rate rises above baseline indicate sympathetic nervous system activation from pain. Sensitivity is configurable per patient.
HRV Suppression = Stress/Pain
Heart rate variability (RMSSD) drops under pain and stress. Lower HRV relative to baseline contributes to the pain score.
RR Interval Tracking
Beat-to-beat interval monitoring enables real-time HRV computation with a rolling window of recent intervals.
Calibrated Baseline
HR baseline accounts for current pain level at calibration time — estimated resting HR is computed by subtracting pain component.
Modality 3
Body Motion Analysis
MediaPipe Pose tracks 33 body landmarks to detect four pain-related movement behaviors
Guarding
Protecting a body area with hands or arms. Detected by tracking hand proximity to torso and crossed-arm postures.
Bracing
Rigid posture and stiffness. Measured by abnormally low joint angle variance indicating tense, locked positioning.
Restlessness
Excessive shifting and fidgeting. Detected via high aggregate motion across all tracked body landmarks over a time window.
Freezing
Abnormally low movement — pain avoidance. When motion drops significantly below the calibrated baseline, it signals distress.
Score Fusion
Multi-Modal Fusion
Three modalities combine into a single pain score with proportional normalization
Face 50%
HR 30%
Body 20%
totalScore = face × 0.50 + hr × 0.30 + body × 0.20    (sum = 1.0)
Proportional Normalization
When a modality has no data (e.g. no HR sensor), the remaining weights scale up proportionally to maintain sum = 1.0
Adjustable Sliders
Clinicians can adjust the weight of each modality via sliders. Changing one auto-redistributes the others.
Lock Toggles
Lock any slider to prevent redistribution. Locked weights stay fixed while unlocked ones adjust around them.
EXAMPLE: NO HR SENSOR CONNECTED
Face weight auto-adjusts to 62.5% and Body to 37.5% — maintaining the original 5:2 ratio while HR is unavailable.

“We don’t assume pain-free. We capture the patient as they are.”

~45 frames captured for neutral face reference
HR Baseline

Body Auto-Cal
Pose baseline from first ~30 frames automatically
Track Change
Score = deviation from baseline, anchored bidirectionally
BIDIRECTIONAL ANCHORING
Positive deviation from baseline scales toward 10. Negative deviation (less pain expression) scales toward 0. The baseline pain level is the anchor.
MULTI-MODAL CALIBRATION
Face, HR, and body each calibrate independently. Face and HR are explicit; body auto-calibrates from initial pose detection frames.
